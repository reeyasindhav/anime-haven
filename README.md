Problem Statement:

Anime enthusiasts often struggle with fragmented experience across platforms, forced to jump between dedicated streaming sites, external seasonal tracking tools, and third-party watch history trackers. Existing streaming interfaces frequently feel generic or cluttered, lacking a vibrant, art-driven aesthetic that resonates with anime culture. Furthermore, discovery tools often fail to provide intuitive seasonal calendars or real-time genre filtering, making it difficult for fans to easily track upcoming releases, discover new shows based on community ratings, and maintain an organised viewing history in one cohesive space.
